Experiment:	Gap Size Testing
Reference:	T001
Description:	Assessment of the clamp gap, aiming to improve clamp force and reduce deformation.

Researcher:	Dr Angus Clark
Date:		19/08/2025

Test Details:
Assess gap size from 2mm to 3.5mm in 0.5mm steps.

Test Results:
2.5mm gap provided good balance of clamp force with little clamp deformation. 2mm too small (maximum clamp deformation see at 10Nm), 3+mm unnecessary as enables very high torques by user.
