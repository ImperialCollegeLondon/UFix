Experiment:	Material Testing
Reference:	T005
Description:	Assessment of material clamping performance (PETG vs ABS)

Researcher:	Dr Angus Clark
Date:		08/09/2025

Test Details:
Test clamp performance in single clamp and dual clamp (typical) setups, measuring force required to cause clamp to slip. Tighten clamps to 10Nm. All clamps tested were 1/2" size. Pin used in dual setup was 5mm diameter. Force applied was a 5N preload, followed by a 50N/s ramp until either 5mm motion or 5kN was reached.

Test Results:
PETG and ABS clamps both performed well, showing an average of 1kN and 1.4kN respectively. Lower clamp forces were demonstrated in dual setups (expectedly). More work currently needed for a 'standard' value.
